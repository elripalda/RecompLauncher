# Getting help

## Questions and help with a game

The [Discord](https://discord.gg/FUzqGM2cvf) is the fastest place to ask. Players and developers are there most of the day.

Check the [FAQ](docs/FAQ.md) and [Troubleshooting](docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md) first. A lot of common problems are answered there.

## Reporting a bug in the launcher

The quickest way is **Settings > Beta** in the launcher. It sends your report straight to me and can include your PC's details.

You can also [open an issue](https://github.com/elripalda/RecompLauncher/issues/new/choose). Please include:

- Your launcher version (**Settings > About**)
- Your system: Windows, Linux or Mac, plus your graphics card
- What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead
- A screenshot or clip if you can
- The log, if a game crashed. The crash screen has a button to send it.

## A problem with a game itself

If a recomp crashes, looks wrong or plays badly, that's usually up to the game's developer. Report it on that game's own GitHub page. Every game page in the launcher links to it.

## Security problems

Don't post them in public. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
