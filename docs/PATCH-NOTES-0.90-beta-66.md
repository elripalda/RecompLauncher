# RecompLauncher 0.90 Beta 66

**This is a beta.** Things may change or break while we're in beta. Thanks for your patience.

- **Game pages are cleaner, with rebuilt Updates, Saves, Shaders and Manage tabs.** — Improved by @Xarishark ([#323](https://github.com/elripalda/RecompLauncher/pull/323), [#325](https://github.com/elripalda/RecompLauncher/pull/325), [#326](https://github.com/elripalda/RecompLauncher/pull/326), [#327](https://github.com/elripalda/RecompLauncher/pull/327)).
- **Cloud saves show their status above Play, with an easier choice when both saves changed.** — Added by @Xarishark ([#319](https://github.com/elripalda/RecompLauncher/pull/319)).
- **Settings → Updates can update all your games automatically, and each game can opt out.** — Added by @Xarishark ([#332](https://github.com/elripalda/RecompLauncher/pull/332)).
- **Games can say what they need from your PC, and the launcher tells you when yours is older.** — Added by @Xarishark ([#336](https://github.com/elripalda/RecompLauncher/pull/336)).
- **On Linux every game plays from a plain folder, and games keep their files in one place.** — Improved by @Xarishark ([#310](https://github.com/elripalda/RecompLauncher/pull/310), [#309](https://github.com/elripalda/RecompLauncher/pull/309)).
- **Discover is now Catalog, the scan pill says Safe, and play time counts every PC on your account.** — Improved by @Xarishark ([#329](https://github.com/elripalda/RecompLauncher/pull/329), [#291](https://github.com/elripalda/RecompLauncher/pull/291), [#328](https://github.com/elripalda/RecompLauncher/pull/328)).

Keep the logs coming from the crash window and Settings → Beta. Every one helps.
