# RecompLauncher 0.90 Beta 20

- **Game Settings are back** for the games that lost them.
- **Found in your folders is smarter.** It tells discs apart by their ID, so another game's disc isn't taken for yours.
- **Covers show up** even when one of our sites can't be reached from your connection.
- **Steadier pages while downloading.** Nothing jumps around when several downloads run.
- **Smarter installs.** The launcher starts the game, not its build tools, installs Windows Installer packages, starts a Linux build's own program, and finds the newest release when one was taken down.
- **Clearer messages** when Windows holds on to a file, and when cloud saves are paused.
- **General fixes and optimization.**

Keep the logs coming from the crash window and Settings → Beta. Every one helps.
