# RecompLauncher 0.90 Beta 18

- **Project status on game pages.** See when a recomp has ended, is on hold, or hasn't had an update in a while.
- **I Already Have It.** Add a recomp you already have right from its page, and play it where it is or move it into your Recomp Library.
- **Ultrawide and super-ultrawide.** Game Settings can match your screen and follow your monitor when it changes.
- **Better Linux and Proton support.** A clearer Runs On filter, player reports that show Proton, and Wine Tools to add what a Windows game needs.
- **Smarter checks before you play.** The launcher checks that your PC can run a recomp and that your copy of the game is the right one.
- **More than one save folder per game**, for launchers with several games inside.
- **Hide games from Discover.** New recomps also show up sooner.
- **Achievements now sits at the bottom of the sidebar.**
- **General fixes and optimization.**

Keep the logs coming from the crash window and Settings → Beta. Every one helps.
