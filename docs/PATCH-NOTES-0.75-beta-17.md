# RecompLauncher 0.75 Beta 17

- **A new Big Picture Home.**
  - Your games sit in one row along the bottom, all in view at once.
  - The one you're on fills the screen above it, with its logo, what it's based on, and A to play or Y for its page.
  - Press down for the rest of Home: new recomps, updates and news for your games.
- **Pin to Home.** Pin any game with X and Home shows your pinned games, in the order you like. Press X on a pinned game and pick Move to change its place. Switch Home between Continue Playing and Pinned Games in Settings.
- **Options on every game.** Press X on any game, anywhere in Big Picture mode, for everything you can do with it: play, its page, saves, settings, screenshots, Add to Steam, older releases, its folder, and Uninstall.
- **Storage and Saves in Big Picture.** Settings has a Storage page with how much room each game takes, where you can uninstall, and a Saves page to back up or put back any game's saves.
- **Every game in its own colours.** Pages take their colours from the game's cover, over a soft, blurred backdrop of its art.
- **Cover Flow, rebuilt.**
  - It glides however fast you move.
  - Each case has a real shadow and a reflection on the floor.
  - Opening a game flips its case over into the game's page, and going back flips it home again.
- **Smoother everywhere.** The highlight slides between sections, pages blend into each other, and games open out of their cover and close back into it.
- **Settings with a stick.** Choices side by side (Size, Theme, Library View, Home Shows) change with left and right.
- **Light theme.** Settings → Appearance now has Dark, OLED and Light, in the launcher and in Big Picture mode.
- **Library tile size.** A slider at the bottom right of the Library makes the covers as small or as big as you like.
- **About in Big Picture.** At the bottom of Settings, with codes to scan for the Discord and recomplauncher.com.
- **Downloads that keep going.** A download that stalls picks up again from where it got to, and Install never sits on Getting Ready.
- **Games that build themselves.** Recomps whose setup works from their project files now get those files with the install.
- **Smarter setup.** Games that build themselves with their own setup now always run that setup first, then start the game it makes.
- **Better help when a game closes.** New hints for a disc image of the wrong game, games that need their title update, and graphics cards too old for Vulkan.
- **Smoother controller use.** Presses in Big Picture mode only ever go where you're looking, and Library filters go straight back to All when the last game in one is done.

Keep the logs coming from the crash window and Settings → Beta. Every one helps.
