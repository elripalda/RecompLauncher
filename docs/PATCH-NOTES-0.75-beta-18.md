# RecompLauncher 0.75 Beta 18

- **Discord status.** Discord now shows the game you're playing, with its cover and how long you've been playing.
- **Cover Flow, rebuilt.**
  - Bigger covers sit side by side on a shelf, each one turned slightly toward the middle, with the one you're on up front and biggest.
  - Every game is a solid block of tinted glass, its cover set into the front and its reflection on the floor. The one you're on stands turned a touch so you can see its depth, and turns further with the right stick or the mouse.
  - Under the shelf you see the game's own name, in big letters.
  - Smooth scrolling however fast you go.
- **Flying into a game.** Opening a game from Home, Cover Flow or any cover flies the camera into its cover and out onto the game's page, and going back flies you out to the cover you came from.
- **Setup Required.** Some recomps need a few things on your PC before they play, like Python or Microsoft's build tools, or a long first build. They now carry a yellow Setup Required badge, and once installed their main button is a yellow **Set Up** instead of Play.
  - Set Up shows the developer's steps in order, with buttons to open the README and the game's folder.
  - When you're done, press **Done, Play** and Play is back for good.
  - Before you install one, the yellow Setup Required button on its page shows the same steps, so you know what it needs first.
  - Shortcuts, Steam and the tray open Set Up too, until it's done. It works in Big Picture mode with a controller.
- **Lots more games.** The catalog has grown to nearly 600 PC recomps, with new consoles along the way.
- **New backdrops.** Home and the Library have the launcher's own moving backdrop, lit brighter in the colors of the game you're on. Pick Aurora, Grid or Drift in Settings → Backdrop, or Game Art for the game's own picture. Game pages show their art, sharp.
- **A bigger Home.** Bigger covers along the bottom and bigger game logos.
- **Home Row.** Settings → Home Row shows your games on Home as covers or as wide pictures.
- **Your Library, your way.** Right-click any game to rename it or give it your own cover, and turn on Settings → Appearance → Covers Only to hide the names under covers.
- **Button Icons.** Pick Xbox or PlayStation buttons, or leave it on Automatic to follow your controller.
- **Ready to Play** always shows its checks in green.
- **A smoother way in.** Big Picture mode fades straight into its start-up screen, and fades out cleanly when you leave.
- **Support us.** A Support Us button with a heart in Settings and About takes you to our Ko-fi page.
- **Updates that land.** Updating the launcher closes every open copy first, so the new version always installs, games on a pre-release update to their newest version, and installs and updates go straight from Getting ready to done.
- **Lighter on handhelds.** Big Picture mode does less work on handhelds and with Lighter Launcher on, and covers no longer redraw in the background once their picture is in.
- **Your files, already in place.** A game whose files you've already put where it reads them is ready to play, with nothing to pick.
- **More consoles.** Picking your copy shows the right files for NES, Game Boy, 3DS, Virtual Boy, Genesis, 32X, Game Gear, arcade, CD-i and Amiga recomps, and Add Games You Have finds those games in your folders, Switch games included.
- **Games that build themselves** now find the game their setup made, wherever you told it to go.
- **Signing in.** When the website can't be reached, the launcher tries again, and Settings → Account has a Try Again button that shows what went wrong.
- **Better help when a game closes.** New hints for a ROM or disc version the game doesn't support, for graphics cards that run out of memory, and for games that need Microsoft .NET installed.

Keep the logs coming from the crash window and Settings → Beta. Every one helps.
