# RecompLauncher 0.90 Beta 21

- **Make every game yours.** Customize is now on every game, installed or not. Use your own cover, background, logo and icon from your PC, and they stay with the game, even if you uninstall and come back.
- **Your own description.** Write a few words about any game on the new Info tab, and they show on its page.
- **Logos, see-through.** Pick a transparent logo from SteamGridDB for the background on the game page.
- **Search that stays with you.** Scroll down Discover and the search and filters stay pinned at the top, with the rest one click away under Filter.
- **Easier Add From GitHub.** Type a bit of a game's name and pick it from the list, no exact name needed. The art gallery is easier to browse, with covers, backgrounds and logos side by side, a search for another name, and your own pictures.
- **Don't Ask Again** on the cloud saves question, with a switch in Settings → Account to turn it back on.
- **General fixes and optimization.**

Keep the logs coming from the crash window and Settings → Beta. Every one helps.
