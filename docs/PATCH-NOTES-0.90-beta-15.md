# RecompLauncher 0.90 Beta 15

- **New options for game settings.** Settings can come in groups, with advanced ones tucked away until you want them, and sliders for numbers.
- **Better problem reports.** Reports now carry what happened, your PC and the game's log, so developers can act on them. Signed in, you'll see their answers under Settings → Account.
- **General fixes and optimization.**

Keep the logs coming from the crash window and Settings → Beta. Every one helps.
