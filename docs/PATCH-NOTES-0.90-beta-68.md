# RecompLauncher 0.90 Beta 68

**This is a beta.** Things may change or break while we're in beta. Thanks for your patience.

A big cleanup under the hood: one simpler setup for every game, and fewer ways to get stuck.

- **One Easy Setup for every game.** Your files are checked when you pick them, and a game is only marked set up once its setup works. — Improved by @Xarishark ([#429](https://github.com/elripalda/RecompLauncher/pull/429), [#396](https://github.com/elripalda/RecompLauncher/pull/396), [#392](https://github.com/elripalda/RecompLauncher/pull/392)).
- **Every new release builds again,** so nobody is left on an old build. Build Again After Updates on a game's Manage tab can do it in the background. — Improved by @Xarishark ([#429](https://github.com/elripalda/RecompLauncher/pull/429)).
- **Downloads is one list:** what's downloading or building on top, then what's waiting its turn. — Improved by @Xarishark ([#430](https://github.com/elripalda/RecompLauncher/pull/430), [#431](https://github.com/elripalda/RecompLauncher/pull/431), [#398](https://github.com/elripalda/RecompLauncher/pull/398)).
- **Recomps show their own project art,** and Bring Your Recomps is in Settings. — Improved by @Xarishark ([#400](https://github.com/elripalda/RecompLauncher/pull/400), [#397](https://github.com/elripalda/RecompLauncher/pull/397)).
- **Setup steps for Windows-only games run on Linux and the Steam Deck,** plus improvements to sponsor and partner spots and general fixes. — Improved ([#429](https://github.com/elripalda/RecompLauncher/pull/429), [#411](https://github.com/elripalda/RecompLauncher/pull/411), [#413](https://github.com/elripalda/RecompLauncher/pull/413)).

Keep the logs coming from the crash window and Settings → Beta. Every one helps.
