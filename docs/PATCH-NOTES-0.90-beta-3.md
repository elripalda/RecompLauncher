# RecompLauncher 0.90 Beta 3

- **You choose what's shared.** RecompLauncher asks once whether to share usage and diagnostics or anonymous stats, and both stay off unless you say yes. You can change your answer any time in Settings → General.
- **Your IDs.** Settings → General shows the IDs your data is sent under, with Copy and Reset.
- **Safer sign-in.** On Windows, your sign-in is now sealed with Windows' own protection. On Linux, only you can read it.
- **Add from the cover.** Point at a game in Discover and press + to add it to your Library. Games you've added carry an In Library badge.
- **Found games land in your Library.** When the launcher finds your game files, their recomps go straight into your Library, ready to install.
- **Add and Install.** Both are in the right-click menu, and the page stays right where you were.
- **A scroll bar you can see.** Long pages show where you are.
- **Menus that fit.** The right-click menu fits any window size and scrolls when it needs to.
- **Bring Your Recomps.** Import the recomps you already have from first-time setup or the Library.
- **Download history.** Click a game in it to open its page.
- **Your own background.** Set a background picture for any game from its right-click menu.
- **Games stay in your Library.** A game whose folder isn't there right now (a drive that isn't connected, say) shows as Missing, with Try Again and Find Its Folder. A library file that got damaged is read back, game by game.
- **No more waiting on Getting Ready.** It gives up after a minute and says why, and Cancel works while it waits.
- **Setups that finish.** If a game's own setup didn't finish its work, it opens again instead of the game closing at once.
- **Clearer setup.** Pick a .7z or .zip as your game copy and the launcher tells you to unpack it first. Recomps that build on your PC show whether Visual Studio's C++ tools are installed.
- **Linux.** Links and folders open, Add to Steam works with every kind of Steam install, and a recomp's brand-new Linux build is picked up right away.
- **Big Picture keyboard.** Y types a space and X deletes, like on a console.

Keep the logs coming from the crash window and Settings → Beta. Every one helps.
