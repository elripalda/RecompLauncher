# RecompLauncher 0.5.0

A new look, and setting up a game is now part of installing it.

## Drop in your disc

- Recomps that support Easy setup now set themselves up. Install one, drop in your disc image
  (or pick it or its folder), and watch it get read, checked and set up. Then choose Play or
  look through its settings first.
- The launcher finds the games you already have. Add your ROM and disc image folders in Settings
  and it recognises disc images by what's on them, not by their file names. Games it finds show
  on Home under Found in your folders, ready to install.
- 2014 FIFA World Cup and Streetball Revival are the first two. FIFA's settings (window,
  resolution, frame rate, upscaling, texture filtering and more) live on its page, and its quit
  shortcut shows the right buttons for your controller.

## A new look

- Graphite and cyan, with a sidebar that collapses to icons. Pin any game to it.
- Home has a featured banner and rows you flip through: Jump back in, Popular right now, Just
  added and more.
- Discover has a big search, sorting by Popular, Newest, Most stars or A to Z, and quieter
  console and genre filters.
- A smaller, tidier Library with a Play button on every cover.
- Game pages lead with the recomp's name and what it's based on. Switching between Overview,
  Updates, Settings and Mods no longer redraws the whole page.
- Downloads show in a small bar at the bottom wherever you are.
- Pure black mode for OLED screens.
- Pages fill any window, ultrawide included, and the sidebar tucks itself in on small ones.

## More control

- Right-click any game for automatic updates, launch options, changing your copy, the changelog,
  its readme and more.
- Refresh Discover from Settings whenever you like.

## TV mode

- The same graphite look, with each cover's own colour glowing behind it as you move.
- A full-screen setup with a spinning disc and a progress bar for each step.
- Game settings show the one you're on large, with every choice as a dot.

Thanks for testing. If anything goes wrong, send the log from the crash screen; every one helps.
