# RecompLauncher 0.75 Beta 12

- **The launcher unpacks your disc for you.** Recomps that need your game's extracted files now take the disc image as well. Hand over the .iso and the launcher unpacks it once, right where the game reads it. Works with Xbox, Xbox 360, PlayStation 2 and PSP disc images.
- **More games with one-time setup.** More recomps now use the "insert your copy" screen: pick your disc image or game folder once and the launcher checks it and sets everything up.
- **A disc that looks like a disc.** The setup screen shows your game's cover across the whole disc, with the shine and the hub in the middle, on the desktop and in Big Picture.
- **File browser upgrades.**
  - **Refresh** shows files you just added, without restarting. F5 works too.
  - **Pin** any folder to keep it at the top of the side.
  - **Extract here** unpacks a zip, 7z or rar right from the browser and opens the folder.
- **Clearer setup descriptions.** Each game says in plain words which copy it needs.

Keep the reports coming from the crash window and Settings → Beta. Every one helps.
