# RecompLauncher 0.7.0

A proper first start, your own language, GameBanana mods and a file browser that works with a
controller.

## Setup

- The first time the launcher opens, it walks you through setup like a new console: your language,
  how you'll play, the folders where you keep your games, and where recomps go. It shows the games
  it found that have recomps before you've finished.
- Choose "At a desk" and the launcher opens in a window. Choose "On a TV" and it opens in TV mode
  every time.
- Hold Menu on your controller to switch to TV mode from the desktop.

## Languages

- The launcher is in 23 languages, the same ones as recomplauncher.com. It starts in the language
  your computer uses, and you can change it in Settings.
- The quick menu over a game follows your language too.

## Mods from GameBanana

- Unleashed Recompiled, Marathon Recompiled, Ship of Harkinian, Starship, SpaghettiKart and
  Ghostship get a Mods tab, with the most popular, newest and most liked mods from GameBanana and a
  search.
- Zelda 64: Recompiled and Banjo: Recompiled now have both Thunderstore and GameBanana.
- Open a mod to see its pictures, description and files, then install it in one click. Unleashed
  and Marathon mods are turned on for you.
- Only files that passed GameBanana's virus scan install from the launcher. Zip, 7z and rar all
  work.
- In TV mode, mods from either site are on the game's Mods page; X switches between them.

## File browser

- Picking a disc image, a ROM folder or where games go now opens the launcher's own file browser
  instead of the Windows one.
- Your game folders, Desktop, Documents, Downloads and every drive (with its free space) are at the
  side. Disc images and ROMs that have recomps are marked with the game's name.
- In TV mode it's made for a controller: B goes up a folder, X picks the folder you're in and Y
  jumps to the side.
- The Windows picker is still a click away if you want it.

## For recomp developers

- Add a `mods` entry to `recomplauncher.json` to give your game a Mods tab, from Thunderstore or
  GameBanana. The developer guide has the details.

Thanks for testing. If something looks wrong in your language, a mod won't install, or setup
doesn't find your games, send the log from the crash screen or a report from the game's page; every
one helps.
