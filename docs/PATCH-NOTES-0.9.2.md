# RecompLauncher 0.9.2

## Bring the recomps you already have

- Already have recomps added to Steam, installed with Quiver Launcher, or sitting in a folder? The
  launcher finds them and adds them to your Library.
- They stay where they are and update from here, in the same folder, so your Steam shortcuts keep
  working. Saves are backed up before every update, like always.
- Coming from Quiver Launcher? Your games come over with the version they're on, and you can move
  them into your Recomp Library in the same step.
- The first time you open 0.9.2 it looks on its own and lets you know what it found. After that,
  it's Add games you have in the Library, or Settings, Games and folders.

Thanks for testing. If something breaks, send the log from the crash screen or a report from the
game's page; every one helps.
