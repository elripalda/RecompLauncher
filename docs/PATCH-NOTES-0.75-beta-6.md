# RecompLauncher 0.75 Beta 6

Fixes from your Beta reports.

- **Save backups finish.** Looking for a game's saves gives up on a folder that's far too big or too slow to read, and skips folder links, so Back up now and backups before updates don't spin for ages. If another backup is still running, it tells you instead of waiting forever.
- **No more scrolling on its own.** A controller, wheel or stick that sits pushed to one side no longer keeps moving the selection up or down. It counts once it's back in the middle.
- **Games that ask where your files are.** Some recomps (Forza Motorsport 4, Project Gotham Racing 4, Kameo, Naruto: The Broken Bond) need to be told where your game files are. When one closes because of that, the crash window has a Pick your game files button. Pick the folder once and it starts from there.

Keep the reports coming from the crash window and Settings → Beta. Every one helps.
