# RecompLauncher 0.75 Beta 20

- **Achievements, a sneak peek.** A new Achievements tab, on the desktop and in Big Picture mode, gives a first look at what's coming: achievements that unlock while you play, your profile and rating, and leaderboards. Sign in now and they'll be tied to your account.
- **Game tags.** Every game page shows what a recomp offers, like Widescreen, 60 FPS, Mods or Online. Click a tag to see every recomp that has it, and filter Discover by tag.
- **About the game.** Game pages have a new card about the original game: what it's about, when it came out, who made it, its series and a critic and player score.
- **More like this.** At the bottom of every game page, recomps you might like next, picked by what they have in common.
- **Wide tiles.** The button next to the cover size slider switches your Library to wide tiles: the game's art with its logo on top. Resizing covers is smooth now, the control stays put at the bottom right, and you can hide it in Settings → Appearance.
- **Cover Flow.** The game you're on stands straight and front and center.
- **Big Picture game pages.** Tags, the About the Game card and More Like This are all a press away with the controller, and moving back up always brings the top of the page back into view.
- **Dropdowns.** Long lists in dropdowns scroll with the launcher's own slim scrollbar.
- **The same filters everywhere.** Discover on the desktop and in Big Picture mode have the same filters, plus new ones: Show (Installed, Not Installed, In Your Library, Setup Required), Recently Updated and Plays Great.
- **Better matches for your games.** Add Games You Have only matches a game with a recomp for the same console and the same game.
- **Smoother all round.** Faster controller response in Big Picture mode, plus lots of optimization and polish across the launcher.

Keep the logs coming from the crash window and Settings → Beta. Every one helps.
