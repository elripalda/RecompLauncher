# RecompLauncher 0.9.1

## Updates without the website

- When a new RecompLauncher is out, it tells you. Press Update now and it downloads, installs and
  opens again by itself.
- The first time a new version opens, What's new shows what changed.

## Get it on my PC

- Signed in on recomplauncher.com, a game's page has a Get it on my PC button. Pick one of your PCs
  and the launcher on it starts installing the game, even if it's sitting by the clock.
- Only PCs that can run that recomp are listed.

## Betas

- Some recomps have a beta: their test builds, before the normal release. A game's page shows it
  when there is one.
- Join (or ask to join), then Play the beta. Back to the release is one button.
- Settings, Account lists the betas you're in. Your account page on recomplauncher.com has them too.
- Works in Big Picture mode, from a game's More menu.

## How did it run?

- After you play a game for 10 minutes or more, a small card asks how it ran: Plays great, Has
  problems or Won't run, with room for a note.
- Your answer shows on the game's page with your graphics card, processor, memory and Windows
  version, so others know what to expect on a PC like theirs. It asks once per version.

## Usage and diagnostics

- The launcher can send which recomps get played, your PC's parts, and what went wrong when a game
  closes right after starting or an install fails. It helps find problems faster.
- On by default. Turn it off in Settings, General, or in first-time setup. What's sent is on
  recompstore.com/privacy.

Thanks for testing. If something breaks, send the log from the crash screen or a report from the
game's page; every one helps.
