# Code of Conduct

RecompLauncher is a community project, and everyone should feel welcome here, whatever their background or experience.

## What we expect

- Be kind and respectful, even when you disagree.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- Help newcomers. Everyone started somewhere.
- Respect the developers whose recomps are listed. They make these in their own time.

## What isn't okay

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks.
- Sexual content or unwanted advances.
- Posting someone's private information without their permission.
- Sharing or asking for game files, ROMs, keys or links to pirated copies.
- Trolling, spam, or anything else that makes the space worse for others.

## Where this applies

Here on GitHub, in the [Discord](https://discord.gg/FUzqGM2cvf), and anywhere else you're representing the project.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, message a moderator on Discord or report it privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/elripalda/RecompLauncher/security/advisories/new). Reports are kept confidential.

Depending on what happened, the response can be a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban from the project's spaces.
